## 2. What personal data we collect

### 2.1 Account and identity

- **Email address** — When you sign up or use “Sign in with” (e.g. Google, Apple, Facebook).
- **User ID** — A unique identifier for your account (UUID).
- **Account type** — Personal or Business, as chosen at sign-up.
- **Subscription status** — Whether you use free or paid features.

### 2.2 Profile information

Depending on your account type and which parts of the app you use:

- **Personal profiles:** Name, gender, date of birth, city, education, occupation, languages, bio, photos, interests, lifestyle and preference details (e.g. for Romance, Friends, or Business mode), and optional links such as Instagram.
- **Business profiles:** Business name, location, bio, tags, website, social links, and logo.

You choose what to add; we store what you provide to show your profile to other users and to power features (e.g. discovery, matching, AI suggestions).

**Date of birth and age.** We store your date of birth to confirm you are 18 or older and to calculate your age. Other users only ever see your **age**, never your date of birth.

### 2.3 Communications and content

- **Messages** — Text, images, and other content you send in chats (direct, group, event).
- **Planner data** — Titles, descriptions, dates, locations, and participants for your plans.
- **Events** — Event details you create or join.

### 2.4 Technical and usage data

- **Session and auth data** — Stored so you stay logged in.
- **Analytics** — We use analytics (e.g. PostHog) to understand how the app is used, **only after you consent**. We do **not** send your name, email, or message content. You can use the app with analytics off.
- **AI usage.** When you use Winkly AI, we send only allow-listed information (e.g. mode, city, date range, activity hints, and a short profile summary) to our AI providers for generating suggestions. We do **not** send your name, contacts, messages, or exact location, and we do **not** use your chat history to train external AI models. *Winkly's suggestions are generated by AI and may be imperfect — always use your own judgement, especially for safety.*

### 2.5 Device and permissions

- **Location** — If you grant permission, your position is **approximated before it is stored** (your precise GPS is never saved); other users only see a rounded distance, never your coordinates. You control precision in Privacy & Safety → Location precision and can revoke permission in device settings.
- **Photos / camera** — Used only when you add or change photos.
- **Contacts** — Only if you choose “find contacts on Winkly”; contacts are **hashed on your device** (SHA-256) before anything is sent, and we do **not** store your contact list.

### 2.6 Optional sensitive information (religion)

You may, entirely voluntarily, add **religion** to your profile. This is a special category of personal data under GDPR Art. 9. We process and display it **only on the basis of your explicit consent** (Art. 9(2)(a)), which we capture through the data-use notice before you enter any personal data (see section 3). You can remove it at any time, which withdraws that consent for the future. If you do not add it, we do not process it. (The former “allergies” field has been removed entirely.)

We do **not** sell your personal data.

| Processor | Purpose | Data categories | Location / transfer basis |

## 8. Your rights

If you are in the EEA or UK you have the rights to: access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection (Art. 21), and to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)). You can export or delete your data in the app (General settings → Account) or contact **privacy@mywinkly.de**; we respond within the time the law requires (normally one month).

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the **Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA)**, Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany — https://www.lda.bayern.de. You may also contact the authority in your own country of residence.

If you are in **California** or a similar jurisdiction: we do not sell personal data, and you may have rights to know, delete, or correct your data and to non-discrimination; contact us to exercise them.

## 11. Security

We use technical and organisational measures including: access control (row-level security so you can only access your own and permitted data); encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and encryption of sensitive tokens at rest; storing only a coarsened version of location (never exact GPS); restricting the profile data sent to AI providers to a minimal allow-list; and rate-limiting and abuse protections. No system is completely secure; we will notify you and the relevant authority of a breach where the law requires.
